# Banana-leaf published-line content replay, 2026-09-28

[Chan et al.'s openly readable 2013 article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/pbi.12015) reports five banana **leaf** line means in Table 1, not fruit measurements. The archived table extract records 154.2 to 257.6 ng/g fresh leaf (1.6706-fold across five line means), plus matching total-soluble-protein figures. For the article's 50 g fresh-leaf oral portion, multiplying those endpoint means gives 7.71 to 12.88 µg, consistent with its rounded 7.7 to 12.9 µg statement. A descriptive coefficient of variation across the five printed line means is 0.2375; it is **not** within-fruit, within-line or digestion-to-digestion variance. The printed ± terms are carried without assigning SD or SEM because their precise meaning was not confirmed. No fruit protein dose or post-digestion intact fraction is reported in these extracted records, so the locked banana-fruit predictor remains unevaluable; this paper adds source-grounded line-level context only. No efficacy inference, construct advice, benchmark win or gate credit.
