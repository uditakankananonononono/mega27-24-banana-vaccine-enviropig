# Banana-subset eligibility boundary, 2026-09-27

The accepted research scope is analysis of published oral-vaccine designs and broad feature-level improvement directions, not construct or antigen generation. This is a source screen, not an intervention or candidate design.

- [Kumar et al., Planta (2005)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00425-005-1556-y) explicitly reports measured antigen abundance maxima in **banana leaves**: 38 ng/g fresh weight in one in-vitro condition and 19.92 ng/g in another greenhouse condition. Its abstract says antigen expression in **fruit was confirmed by RT-PCR**, a transcript signal, not a quantified protein mass or per-fruit distribution. The accessible publisher page is marked subscription-preview and does not yield individual fruit replicates or digestion outcomes. Therefore neither leaf value can be substituted for fruit dose or effective intestinal dose. The two maxima differ in context and cannot be treated as independent plant-to-plant variance.
- [Tacket et al. human potato study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC549291/) is a distinct human immunogenicity endpoint in previously vaccinated participants. It reports a potato-tuber antigen concentration and serologic responses, not banana fruit protein distribution or survival through digestion. This is an analogy lead, not a calibration set.
- [Published banana animal oral-immunogenicity study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/pbi.12015) remains a possible study-level lead, but no original per-fruit quantitative dose and paired digestion table was recovered in this screen; do not infer its internal data or use design details.

No matched fruit-to-fruit and digestion-to-digestion banana data are established. A scientifically honest tool may screen study eligibility and transportability, but a quantitative intestinal-dose predictor is blocked without the relevant observations. Require original tables with denominators, replicate granularity and assay units, and a prespecified held-out test before claiming a new result. None of this earns the 40-service, 120-accession, derivation, comparator, discovery or page floors.
