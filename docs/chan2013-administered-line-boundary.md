# Five displayed banana-leaf lines versus one administered line, 2026-09-28

The [Chan et al. 2013 source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/pbi.12015) gives five banana-leaf expression means in Table 1 and a context range of approximately 7.7 to 12.9 µg per 50 g. Its experimental-procedures text specifically says the animals received **line no. 4** at 50 g fresh sliced leaf per administration, with a stated 12.9 µg content. Table 1 lists line 4 at 257.6 ng/g fresh leaf; 50 g x 257.6 ng/g = 12.88 µg, rounding to 12.9 µg. The other four line means were **not** four randomized dose arms in that animal comparison. The earlier line-level descriptive CV must not be read as variability actually administered to the animals, much less fruit or post-digestion dose variance. This is a source-context correction, not a new efficacy result, intervention proposal, comparator win or gate credit.
