# Numeric GI vaccine-antigen evidence, but not the locked banana-fruit endpoint

[Berardi's 2013 thesis](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/47928/) contains numerical GI results not recovered in the screened [2017 accepted manuscript](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/78842/). This corrects an overly broad impression that the Berardi research line offers only qualitative gels: the thesis *does* report numeric antigen-related recovery, but under different formulations, timing and denominator. It is a thesis from the same researcher, not automatically an independent validation publication.

In the [original 299-page PDF](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/47928/1/2013BerardiAPhD.pdf), Table 5.6 (downloaded PDF page 205, thesis page 189) reports HBcAg native virus-like particle release after gastric exposure from four microparticle conditions, **6.0 ± 1.2%, 11.3 ± 0.9%, 2.7 ± 0.7%, 4.1 ± 0.5%**, with n=3 and SDs. Figure 4.11's associated passage (downloaded PDF page 173, thesis page 157) reports **87.1 ± 9.6%** native VLP release from coated tablets after two hours simulated gastric exposure followed by intestinal-fluid exposure, normalized to tablets incubated in intestinal fluid without prior gastric exposure. Direct ELISA gives 91.4 ± 4.3% under that source-defined assay. The sandwich assay detects native VLP, while direct ELISA has broader antigen detection. The two formulation numbers cannot be pooled, compared causally or treated as replicate-level values.

These measurements support a measurable GI-stage antigen readout and a sharp provenance/denominator distinction. They are not banana fruit or between-fruit dose observations; the tablet value is **intestinal release after a prior gastric step relative to a no-gastric control**, not a direct assayed fraction of all original antigen remaining intact at the end of digestion. The thesis does not supply an independently held-out same-task banana publication or raw per-replicate fractions for the locked predictor. Thus the pre-registered primary model is still unevaluable. The original six-paper screen remains a six-*paper* screen; this thesis is a related supplemental source, not a seventh independent study. We archived the PDF with SHA-256, matched original text and table labels in a replay test, and visually inspected its actual downloaded pages 173 and 205. No construct or formulation advice is made. No service, accession, derivation, 50-page substantive paper or benchmark/discovery gate is credited.
