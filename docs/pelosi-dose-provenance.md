# Pelosi 2012 numeric dose records: pinned provenance, still not the locked endpoint

The [Pelosi et al. 2012 sheep study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3527624/) is now archived as [Europe PMC full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC3527624/fullTextXML) with SHA-256 pinned and its printed numeric records replayed in `results/pelosi_dose_provenance.json`. The paper prints one antigen content value for both vaccine batches (300 µg/g dry weight, hairy root and leaf), quantified by capture ELISA against a commercial recombinant standard; a formulation of 19 g freeze-dried material in 200 ml oil emulsion per dose; and a stated 5 mg antigen delivery per dose. Arithmetic replay: 19 g x 300 µg/g = 5.7 mg available per dose, covering the stated 5 mg with a 14% margin, so the printed dose chain is internally consistent. Sheep were randomized into four groups of 2-5 animals.

This sharpens the earlier narrative screen entry with exact archived provenance, but the classification stands: the outcomes are animal antibody titres, there is no measured post-digestion intact-antigen fraction, both batches share one printed content value so no between-batch variability can be computed, and group sizes are animal replicates, not plant-to-plant or digestion-to-digestion replicates. Not banana fruit data. The locked primary endpoint remains unevaluable from this source; the eligibility screen count is unchanged. No design, formulation or protocol recommendation is made, and no service, accession, derivation, comparator, discovery or page gate credit is claimed.
