# Five-source dose-denominator ledger, 2026-09-28

This short ledger cross-checks five previously archived and tested source records: [Kumar banana leaves](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00425-005-1556-y), [Thanavala potato tubers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC549291/), [Pelosi dry root/leaf tissue](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3527624/), [Pyrski lettuce processing/storage](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3088802/) and [Chan banana leaf lines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/pbi.12015). Their printed content and retention figures use different tissues, water bases, and stages. Kumar's fruit evidence is transcript detection, not a measured fruit protein mass; the lettuce stage figures cannot be collapsed into an intestinal-delivered fraction. None of these five pinned records has both banana fruit protein mass and a matched post-digestion intact fraction. The scripted JSON table reads the prior audited records and makes these specific source and denominator gaps inspectable. It is not a new independent study, literature-exhaustive absence claim, dose model, intervention suggestion, comparator win, discovery or gate credit.
