# Published endpoint compatibility screen, 2026-09-27

The locked primary endpoint is **measured post-digestion intact-antigen fraction** on an independently held-out publication. It cannot be replaced by a leaf expression maximum, fruit transcript detection, immune response or qualitative gel band. A small reproducible screen now records five published leads and exactly which endpoint each supplies. The script `scripts/eligibility_screen.py`, JSON and replay test enforce this boundary. This screen is incomplete and does not claim the endpoint is absent from all literature.

- [Kumar et al., Planta 2005](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00425-005-1556-y): accessible abstract gives two banana **leaf** antigen maxima under different growth settings and confirms fruit expression by RT-PCR. It does not yield fruit protein quantities, biological fruit-to-fruit variation or direct digestive recovery. The publisher's subscription preview limits this screen.
- [Thanavala et al., 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC549291/): human potato immunogenicity observations are neither banana fruit protein distribution nor direct measured post-digestion intact fraction.
- [Pelosi et al., PLOS ONE 2012](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0052907): original article contrasts sheep immune responses across plant tissues and preparation contexts. That indirect readout cannot be pooled into a measured digestion-survival fraction.
- [Berardi et al., International Journal of Pharmaceutics 2017](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/78842/): repository [accepted manuscript PDF](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/78842/1/Accepted_manuscript%20%282%29.pdf), 36 pages, archived with SHA-256, reports purified plant-expressed antigen stability in simulated gastric and intestinal and pig intestinal fluids through gels/blots/imaging. Its source explicitly distinguishes gastric physical aggregation from polypeptide integrity and qualitative intestinal stability. No numerical replicate-level intact-antigen fraction with uncertainty was recovered for the registered primary model. This is a **measurement-definition lead**, not banana fruit dose data or a poolable numerical observation.
- [Lakshmi et al., PLOS ONE 2013](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0054708) ([PMC3552857](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3552857/)): full-text XML fetched from Europe PMC. It reports lettuce CTB-ESAT6 expression of 11.2 µg/g fresh weight, tobacco expression up to 7.5% of total soluble protein, a 22-fold antigen concentration after lyophilization, and no significant antigen loss in lyophilized leaves after six months of room-temperature storage, plus in vitro GM1-ganglioside binding. Stomach protection is discussed only as qualitative bioencapsulation reasoning; no measured numeric post-digestion intact-antigen fraction, no banana fruit data and no within-fruit replicates are given. It is an honest near-miss: storage stability and expression evidence, not the locked endpoint.

Historical result at five-paper prose stage (the machine-readable file contained only four records then): **0/5 narrative leads** gave the locked primary numeric endpoint or matched banana fruit quantitative protein, fruit-to-fruit replicates and digestion outcome. See the six-record correction below. No trained predictor, holdout benchmark or win can legitimately be claimed from them. A distinct positive experimental report or accessible supplement with compatible denominators, timepoints and replicates could change this; the screen stays open. It earns zero locked project gate credit. Published differences may guide broad measurement-standard recommendations, but no new antigen or construct design follows.

## Follow-up: a numeric digestion endpoint in the wrong therapeutic class

A new [2022 spirulina oral-biologic paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9200632/) reports that **more than 70%** of encapsulated aa682, a therapeutic VHH antibody, remained intact after **2 hours of simulated gastric conditions**, versus purified aa682 fully degraded within **2 minutes**. Its Fig. 6B caption describes capillary electrophoresis immunoassay (CEIA) data representative of four independent experiments. This demonstrates that a numeric, time-defined post-gastric intact-*protein* observation is possible in published work, but it is **not** a vaccine antigen, not banana fruit, and not a per-replicate intact-antigen table for the locked vaccine endpoint. We therefore mark it as a useful measurement-definition near-miss and do not train or claim a comparator victory from it. Source XML SHA-256 is pinned in `results/eligibility_screen.json`.

Reconciliation: the prior screen narrative listed five papers but the replay script/JSON counted only four (Lakshmi was prose-only). The script now includes Lakshmi and this sixth paper explicitly. Replayed count is **0/6 eligible for the locked antigen endpoint**, not a claim that numeric digestion measurements do not exist in related oral-biologic work. The specific positive observation here is explicitly out of task, and must not be mislabeled as an antigen-survival percentage.

Earlier in this running log the sentence "0/5 screened studies" described the narrative five and is superseded by the six-record replay above. The script's prior four-record JSON was an indexing mistake, not five machine-screened records. The original prose and past commits are retained for audit; use the current `screened_studies` array and test, not the superseded sentence, for the live count.

## Related thesis correction

The [Berardi 2013 thesis PDF](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/47928/1/2013BerardiAPhD.pdf) reports numerical HBcAg GI-stage *native-VLP release* after gastric exposure under defined formulation-specific controls, even though the separately screened 2017 accepted manuscript did not yield a compatible numeric replicate table. See `docs/berardi-thesis-endpoint-denominator.md`: 87.1 ± 9.6% relative intestinal release from coated tablets and four n=3 microparticle release summaries of 2.7%-11.3% are real source-reported measurements, but their denominators differ and neither supplies the locked banana fruit/intact-fraction held-out task. The six-paper screen stays at 0/6 for that specific endpoint; do not describe the whole Berardi research line as only qualitative.
