# Published endpoint compatibility screen, 2026-09-27

The locked primary endpoint is **measured post-digestion intact-antigen fraction** on an independently held-out publication. It cannot be replaced by a leaf expression maximum, fruit transcript detection, immune response or qualitative gel band. A small reproducible screen now records four published leads and exactly which endpoint each supplies. The script `scripts/eligibility_screen.py`, JSON and replay test enforce this boundary. This screen is incomplete and does not claim the endpoint is absent from all literature.

- [Kumar et al., Planta 2005](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00425-005-1556-y): accessible abstract gives two banana **leaf** antigen maxima under different growth settings and confirms fruit expression by RT-PCR. It does not yield fruit protein quantities, biological fruit-to-fruit variation or direct digestive recovery. The publisher's subscription preview limits this screen.
- [Tacket et al., 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC549291/): human potato immunogenicity observations are neither banana fruit protein distribution nor direct measured post-digestion intact fraction.
- [Pelosi et al., PLOS ONE 2012](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0052907): original article contrasts sheep immune responses across plant tissues and preparation contexts. That indirect readout cannot be pooled into a measured digestion-survival fraction.
- [Berardi et al., International Journal of Pharmaceutics 2017](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/78842/): repository [accepted manuscript PDF](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/78842/1/Accepted_manuscript%20%282%29.pdf), 36 pages, archived with SHA-256, reports purified plant-expressed antigen stability in simulated gastric and intestinal and pig intestinal fluids through gels/blots/imaging. Its source explicitly distinguishes gastric physical aggregation from polypeptide integrity and qualitative intestinal stability. No numerical replicate-level intact-antigen fraction with uncertainty was recovered for the registered primary model. This is a **measurement-definition lead**, not banana fruit dose data or a poolable numerical observation.

Result: **0/4 screened studies** currently give the locked primary numeric endpoint, and **0/4** give matched banana fruit quantitative protein, fruit-to-fruit replicates and digestion outcome. No trained predictor, holdout benchmark or win can legitimately be claimed from them. A distinct positive experimental report or accessible supplement with compatible denominators, timepoints and replicates could change this; the screen stays open. It earns zero locked project gate credit. Published differences may guide broad measurement-standard recommendations, but no new antigen or construct design follows.
