## 2026-09-28, rice pivot accession screen (source discovery only)

- **OCV source cohort verified:** Chac et al., *Nature Communications*, DOI [10.1038/s41467-025-67388-y](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67388-y), reports 89 participants in Dhaka, stool metagenomic sequencing on vaccination day and memory B-cell response association. Its named SRA BioProject [PRJNA782606](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA782606) resolves at NCBI as "Gut microbiota at time of cholera vaccination," raw sequence reads, 89 SRA experiments and 89 BioSamples (356 Gbases, 0.11 TB). This establishes discoverable raw reads, **not** yet a verified sample-to-response mapping or reusable frozen score. The article mentions participant metadata in Supplementary Data 6; matching its entries and endpoint definitions to the SRA samples is unverified. Do not download reads under the SLOW-group constraint.
- **Target MucoRice cohort remains a hard access gate:** [Yuki et al., Lancet Microbe 2021](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2820%2930196-8/fulltext) states that baseline fecal metagenomes were assessed in 20 vaccinated people (12 responders, 8 nonresponders), with responder criteria and UniFrac analysis; a separate consent/protocol and appendix pp 24–81 cover the microbiota study. Its accessible article text does **not** identify a raw-read accession or a participant-level feature matrix. Target metagenomic data availability and individual labels have **not** been verified; search results for DRX011151 / 19A accessions refer to rice-plant genomic sequencing, not these human stool specimens. No AUROC prereg or validation claim until the real target sample-level data and permission/access terms are confirmed.
- **Interpretation:** The cross-vaccine transfer is an attractive testable question, not a finding. Outcome definition differs between source OSP-specific MBC response and target CTB-specific antibody response; this biological/endpoint shift is a deliberate transportability challenge requiring an honest comparator and frozen feature mapping, not a pooled-cohort shortcut. If MucoRice target data cannot be found, steer toward another genuinely open independent validation set rather than relabeling 51A, 19A or MTBLS437 as validation. User's latest direction favors rich open public datasets, including NASA GeneLab and ENA/SRA/GEO/CPTAC/TCGA-class repositories; screen source and target accessibility before designing analyses. No heavy compute attempted.

### Follow-up screen, 2026-09-28: apparent second OCV accession is not untouched validation

The later [Chac et al. Nature Communications article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67388-y) expressly describes its 89 participants as adapted from a parent study and cites earlier microbiome findings; whether particular participant IDs overlap between PRJNA742046 and PRJNA782606 needs a documented sample join, but **do not count PRJNA742046 as untouched external validation** merely because the accession differs. A frozen shotgun-derived gene-level signature would also not transfer without a defensible assay-feature bridge into 16S data. These are exclusion/compatibility facts, not another negative endpoint result. The MucoRice target access gate still holds.
